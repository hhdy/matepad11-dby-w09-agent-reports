--- Work package E — first-boot implications and recommendation --------------
RESULT: BLOCKED (no final ABL FDT; conservative gating required)

Classification by category (omission risk is stated, not assumed safe):

  1. Boot-critical before UFS/system first-stage mount / firmware-owned:
     hyp_region, xbl_aop_region, cmd_db, smem_region, removed_region,
     res_xbl_uefi_log_region. These are XBL/ABL/AOP/SMEM/hyp handshake and
     command-db regions. Firmware may already occupy these physical ranges
     before Linux boots; omitting a no-map reservation from a replacement DTB
     can let Linux advertise overlap to a region still owned by firmware.
     Because their HOS4.3 coordinates are redacted here and no final ABL FDT
     proves what was handed off, they CANNOT be safely reconstructed or safely
     dropped by this audit. GATE.
  2. Firmware/ABL-owned PIL carveouts (no-map removed pools):
     pil_* (#7..#18) + cdsp_secure_heap (#19). Omission is acceptable only if
     the corresponding remoteproc/PIL consumer is never enabled; HDR/firmware
     loading of ADSP/CDSP/SLPI/NPU/SPSS/camera would then fail or could overlap
     memory firmware still uses. Deferred in rev2, so not first-boot critical
     for storage-only, but NOT proven safe to permanently omit.
  3. Required when subsystem enabled:
     adsp/sdsp/cdsp (#20-22), qseecom/ta (#32-33), secure_display (#34),
     cnss_wlan (#35), linux,cma (#36), mailbox (#37). Only needed when the
     matching DSP/secure/display/WLAN/CMA/audio driver is enabled. rev2
     defers all of these; a first storage-only boot does not require them,
     but enabling the subsystem without restoring the pool is a defect.
  4. Display/splash/debug:
     cont_splash_region (#23), disp_rdump_region (#24), dfps_data_region (#25),
     rainbow_mem (#29), bootfail_mem (#30), mem_dump_region (#26), kerneldump
     (#31). Vendor diagnostics / splash continuity; omitting may lose splash
     continuity or crash logs. Not required for UFS mount; #25 has an MDSS/DSI
     consumer in the base tree, so a display-enabled kernel must restore it.
  5. Unresolved:
     cdsp_secure_heap size (#19), cont/disp_rdump overlap semantics (both
     nodes in the base text share a redacted unit-address suffix but have
     different size classes), and whether ABL injects any reservation into the
     final FDT. All kept UNKNOWN.

Recommendation: The current minimal rev2 board DTS, which reconstructs no
reserved-memory, is NOT yet acceptable for a full first boot, but the specific
UFS/system first-stage mount path itself shows NO reserved-memory dependency
in the candidate. A conservative storage-only kernel boot could proceed
WITHOUT reproducing the 37 HOS4.3 carveouts only after the final live ABL FDT
is captured and confirms (a) the usable-memory handoff and (b) that firmware
does not rely on Linux to re-declare its regions. Because the final ABL FDT is
unavailable and no local evidence proves ABL adds or removes reservations,
this gate is BLOCKED. Do not invent coordinates, do not copy HOS4.2 or upstream
sm8250.dtsi addresses into the HOS4.3 tree, and do not claim a subsystem is
safe to omit solely because its driver is disabled in rev2.
